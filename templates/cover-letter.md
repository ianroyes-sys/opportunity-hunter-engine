# Cover letter skeleton

Rules: one page max, no "I am writing to express my interest," lead with a result, mirror 2–3 phrases from the posting, close with a specific ask. Every claim must trace to `profile/profile.md`.

---

Dear [Name / "Hiring Team" only if truly unnamed],

**[Hook — one proof point from the profile matched to what they want.]** e.g.: As treasurer of my campus investment club I manage a $25,000 student portfolio, so when I saw [Org]'s [program] emphasizes [their phrase], it read like the work I already do.

**[Body 1 — the most relevant experience, told as a result.]**

**[Body 2 — why THEM, specifically.]** One researched sentence about the org (a product, a program feature, a value) and the bridge to your experience.

**[Close — confident, specific.]** I'd welcome the chance to talk about how I could contribute to [team/program]. I'm available at [email] or [phone].

Sincerely,
[Your name]
